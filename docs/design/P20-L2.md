## 5. Persona journeys that dead-end today

**Platform operator (term start).** Creates a university by CLI. Dead ends: cannot rename it, add an email domain or fix its timezone without SQL (G3); no UI or list of universities (X8); no billing (X4); no metrics or deploy target (X9, K3); SAML campuses cannot sign in at all (X1).

**University admin (before and during a term).** Creates departments and terms, invites staff, imports courses and sections by CSV, edits the grade scale, reads the audit log. Dead ends: cannot roll last term's sections forward (G9); cannot import from or export to the SIS (X2, G8); no reports on enrolment, grade distribution across sections or activity (X29, analytics row of §1); no custom roles for a registrar (X12); no bulk actions on members (X47); invitations cannot be resent (G5); room clashes and double-booked instructors are not detected (X17, X19); privacy requests (export, delete) have no tool (G7).

**Department admin.** Same as admin for own departments. Dead ends: cross-listed courses impossible (X10); lecture-plus-lab structure modelled only as unrelated sections (X11); invites members only.

**Instructor (a full term).** Writes the section home, posts announcements (emailed), uploads weekly materials, builds quizzes and assignments, reviews and grades, finalizes. Dead ends: authors the same content once per section and per term (G15, X33); no rich text, links, video or announcement attachments (X30, X31, X34); cannot schedule content to appear later (X32); quizzes are choice and fill-in only, one attempt, no bank or shuffle (X50-X52); no rubrics, peer review, plagiarism check or group work (X22, X55); no per-student extension or accommodation (X57); no discussion space (X28); cannot add a late-joining student (G4); no attendance (G11); no analytics on who opened materials or is falling behind (X29, G18); grade release and due dates send no mail (X27, X53).

**TA.** Same content powers as the instructor, scores and reviews. Dead ends: as the instructor's, plus no grading queue across sections (each assignment's submissions page is separate, `routes/$slug/sections_.$id.assignments_.$assessmentId_.submissions.tsx`).

**Student (a full term).** Signs in, sees My sections with the schedule and clashes, browses the catalog, reads content, downloads files, takes quizzes, submits assignments, reads scores, feedback and GPA. Dead ends: cannot enrol, drop or join a waitlist from the catalog (X16); no single "what is due" view or reminders (G2, X53, X54); no calendar export (X20); no in-app notifications (G1); cannot ask a question in a forum or message staff (X28, G10); no installable phone app or push (X6, G13); dates follow the browser, not the campus (G12); no PDF transcript (X25); no appeal path beyond asking in person (X24).

**Invited person.** Opens the invite link; the page cannot say which university or role before sign-in (X45); an expired link needs the admin to delete and re-invite (G5).
