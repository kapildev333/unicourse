## 2. 2026 trends, ranked by how many independent sources name them

"Independent" means different publishers. Vendors count once each.

1. **Generative AI built into the instructor workflow** (draft modules, quiz items, rubrics, summaries, feedback) - 7 sources.
   Instructure IgniteAI [S1][S2], D2L Lumi [S13], Anthology AI Design Assistant [S10], Moodle 5.0/5.1 [S6][S8], Google Gemini [S25]; EDUCAUSE Horizon 2026 names instructional design as reshaped by AI [S15]; Tyton 2026 [S17].
2. **Assessment redesign, and a retreat from AI-writing detection** - 6 sources.
   Horizon 2026: AI pushes towards process-based evidence of learning [S15]. Tyton (15 Jun 2026): 47% of faculty redesigned assessments [S17].
   Waterloo dropped Turnitin AI detection (Sep 2025) [S49]; Curtin from 1 Jan 2026 [S50]; Advance HE favours process and feedback over detection (May 2026) [S51]; EDUCAUSE assessment study (Mar 2026 survey) [S52].
3. **Student-facing AI tutors grounded in course material** - 6 sources.
   Google Gemini in Classroom for students (4 Aug 2026) [S24]; D2L Lumi Tutor and Chat (Jun 2026) [S14]; Moodle "Explain" (Apr 2025) [S8]; Blackboard AI Conversation (Jul 2026) [S19].
   University of Alberta Canvas pilot (Sep 2026 - May 2027) answers only from instructor-uploaded material [S26]; University of Zurich teaching assistant pilot (autumn 2026) [S26].
4. **Accessibility as a hard procurement gate** - 6 sources.
   DOJ Title II IFR (20 Apr 2026) [S53]; EU Accessibility Act in force 28 Jun 2025 [S54][S55]; University of Arizona enforces WCAG 2.2 AA on every purchase [S56]; Times Higher Education calls it a leadership issue (2026) [S54]; vendors ship accessibility work (D2L Mar 2026 [S14], Sakai 25 [S23]).
5. **Analytics shifting from reporting to proactive intervention (early alert)** - 5 sources.
   EDUCAUSE Top 10 2026 "From Reactive to Proactive" and two data-literacy items [S16]; Canvas Students in Need of Attention at course level [S48]; D2L Lumi Insights and Performance+ predictive [S13][S33]; Gartner Hype Cycle for Higher Ed 2025/2026 on analytics and personalisation [S57].
6. **Learner-facing overview, progress and notification UX** - 5 sources.
   Moodle 5.1 Activities overview [S6]; Open edX Ulmo progress carousel and email digests [S22]; Google Classroom role-based homepage (21 Jul 2026) [S44]; D2L completion views [S14]; Sakai course cards [S23].
7. **Data privacy and AI governance** - 5 sources.
   Horizon 2026 lists data privacy and regulation as redefining priorities [S15]; EDUCAUSE Top 10 2026 "Knowledge Management for Safer AI" [S16]; 1EdTech Generative AI Data Rubric (privacy section) [S58]; EU AI Act Annex III treats AI grading and exam monitoring as high-risk [S59]; Alberta chose its own assistant to keep data control [S26].
8. **Security and vendor-continuity risk** - 4 sources.
   Canvas cyber incident: intrusion detected 29 Apr 2026, global outage 7-8 May 2026, exams rescheduled [S60][S61]; Anthology Chapter 11 filed 29 Sep 2025, plan confirmed 23 Jan 2026 [S62]; EDUCAUSE Top 10 2026 #1 "Collaborative Cybersecurity" [S16]; On EdTech coverage of trust after the incident [S37].
9. **The AI hype vs use gap; training and human connection** - 4 sources.
   Inside Higher Ed (30 Sep 2026): no dedicated AI tool in the top 100 LTIs, Gemini at 87,788 users; only 11% of instructors comprehensively trained [S39]; Instructure Top 40 (29 Sep 2026) [S38]; EDUCAUSE Top 10 "The Human Edge of AI" [S16]; D2L survey (Oct 2025, n=500) [S63].
10. **AI sold as a tier upsell** - 4 sources.
    Canvas Core / Plus / Next (21 Apr 2026), free AI only until 30 Jun 2026 (US) [S2]; Intelligent Insights moved into Plus/Next [S48]; Brightspace Performance+ and Creator+ add-ons [S33]; Google recording and Gemini tools gated by edition [S25][S44].

    Instructure Top 40: content, video and SIS tools dominate; 28 LTIs per institution [S38]; Open edX reusable LTI configurations [S21]; Sakai LTI group submissions [S23]; 1EdTech Edu-API for SIS-LMS exchange [S64].
12. **Delegated / distributed administration** - 3 sources.
    D2L Distributed Administration (Jan and Jun 2026) [S13][S14]; Open edX granular roles and admin console [S21]; Moodle course- and activity-level AI controls [S6].
13. **Reusable content and question libraries** - 3 sources. Moodle 5.0 shared question banks [S8]; Open edX libraries [S21]; D2L Lumi Remix [S14].
14. **Group work at scale** - 3 sources. D2L group assignments and Groups Management [S13][S14]; Sakai group rubrics [S23]; Google student groups [S45].

## 3. Table stakes vs differentiators, as the sources describe them

The clearest statement comes from a vendor's own packaging. Instructure (21 Apr 2026) defines Canvas Core as "foundational LMS functionality and essential AI features"; Plus adds engagement tools, advanced analytics and teaching/feedback AI; Next adds the newest AI [S2].
D2L draws the same line with Brightspace core vs Performance+ (analytics) and Creator+ (authoring) [S33]; Google with Fundamentals vs Plus editions [S41].

Table stakes (every product in §1 has them, and buyers score them):
- Modules/content, assignments, quizzes, rubrics, gradebook, discussions, announcements, calendar (§1, all five rows are Y or nearly Y).
- Mobile apps: MTSU's 19-person panel scored mobile 6.42 (Canvas) vs 3.16 (Brightspace) on 1-7, and chose Canvas (Spring 2025) [S36].
- LTI 1.3 and SIS integration: the median institution runs 28 LTI tools; content, video and SIS tools fill the top 40 [S38].
- Accessibility conformance with a current VPAT/ACR (§4) [S56].
- Support and price: Villanova chose Brightspace partly on support and a contract $1M cheaper [S66].
- "Essential AI" now sits in the base tier at Canvas [S2]; Moodle ships an AI subsystem in core [S8]. Basic instructor AI is moving into table stakes.

Differentiators (sold separately, gated, or only in one or two products):
- Predictive/early-alert analytics (Canvas Plus/Next, Performance+) [S48][S33].
- Agentic AI that performs multi-step course changes (IgniteAI Agent, early adopter, Mar 2026) [S1].
- Student AI tutors grounded in course material (Lumi Tutor, Gemini, campus pilots) [S14][S24][S26].
- Native video creation and captioning (Studio, Collaborate, Creator+) [S46][S32][S33].
- Native attendance tied to live sessions (Blackboard Collaborate -> Attendance) [S32].
- Course development depth, which Villanova's committee rated highest in Brightspace [S66].

## 5. Cheap-but-expected vs expensive

Judgement of this lane, grounded in §1 and §3. "Cheap" means plain CRUD plus a list or a scheduled job on data an LMS already has; "expensive" means new infrastructure, a model, a vendor contract, or a compliance regime.

Cheap but expected (every incumbent has it; absence is noticed):
- In-app notifications plus an email digest for announcements, new materials, due dates and grades [S22][S40].
- A calendar / to-do view of due dates across sections, with an iCal feed [S6][S44].
- Threaded discussions per section [§1].
- Rubrics attached to assignments, reused across sections [S20][S42].
- Attendance registers per meeting with present/late/absent/excused weights feeding the gradebook (Blackboard's model) [S32][S33].
- Groups within a section and group submissions [S13][S23].
- Peer review on assignments [S34][S35].
- Activity/progress overview per student and per section ("what is due, what is missing") [S6][S14].
- Read tracking on announcements and feedback [S19][S20].
- Bulk date shifting when a section is copied to a new term (Sakai Date Manager) [S23].
- CSV/API export of grades and rosters (and later OneRoster) [S43].
- Simple rule-based "students in need of attention" (missing submissions, low scores, no recent login) [S48].

Expensive (infrastructure, vendor, model or compliance cost):
- Student AI tutoring grounded in course content, with privacy guarantees and EU AI Act exposure if it grades [S24][S26][S59].
- Instructor AI generation (quiz, rubric, content) - cheaper than tutoring, but still model cost, review UX and governance [S1][S8].
- Video hosting, transcoding and captioning to ~99% accuracy (auto-captions reportedly ~85%) [S46].
- Live virtual classroom (Collaborate, BigBlueButton) [S30][S32].
- Proctoring and AI-writing detection: costly and increasingly switched off [S49][S50].
- Predictive analytics and data warehouse feeds (Canvas Data 2, Performance+) [S47][S33].
- LTI 1.3 Advantage platform certification and an app ecosystem [S21][S47].
- Native mobile apps with push notifications [S36].
- Formal WCAG 2.2 AA audit and published ACR/VPAT (cost is the audit, not the code) [S56].

## 6. Open questions for the synthesis lane

1. Which §5 "cheap but expected" items does UniCourse already cover (P20-L2's inventory)? Notifications, calendar/iCal, discussions, attendance and rubrics are the likely gaps.
2. Do we want a stance on AI at all in the next phase, given the allowed Go modules in CLAUDE.md (pgx, x/oauth2, go-oidc, x/crypto)? A provider SDK is not on that list; a plain net/http call is, but cost, data processing terms and EU AI Act exposure are owner decisions.
3. Accessibility: the product's own contrast checks exist (Spec 3). Do we commit to WCAG 2.2 AA and publish an ACR, given procurement now asks for it [S56]?
4. Attendance model: Blackboard-style per-meeting statuses with weights feeding grades, or a simple register outside grades? Our sections already have schedules (Spec 2), which make meetings derivable.
5. Interop priority: LTI 1.3 tool-consumer support (to embed Zoom/Panopto/McGraw Hill, the top LTIs) vs OneRoster/CSV SIS sync. Instructure's data says both matter; which first?
6. Early alert: is a rule-based "needs attention" list (no ML) enough to claim the trend, given vendors gate the predictive version behind paid tiers?
7. Security posture after the Canvas incident: the exploited path was free self-service teacher accounts [S60]. Do we have any self-service sign-up that bypasses a university tenant?
8. Assessment integrity: given the retreat from AI detection, should quizzes/assignments gain process evidence (drafts, staged submissions, version history) rather than detection?
9. Distributed administration maps directly onto our university/department admin split; is anything missing (delegated course templates, sub-org roles)?
10. Market-share numbers for 2026 (On EdTech Spring 2026, 7 May 2026) are paywalled; does the synthesis need them?
